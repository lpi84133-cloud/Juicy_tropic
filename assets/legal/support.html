How to play

Change the weather to ripen and drop fruit, then move the garden drone to catch the harvest. Return to the unloading station when the drone is full.

  • Sun ripens fruit faster.
  • Rain soaks ripe fruit until it falls.
  • Wind knocks ripe fruit from the trees.
  • Storm can turn fruit golden, but makes collecting harder.
