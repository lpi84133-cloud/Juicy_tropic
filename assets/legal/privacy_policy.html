Who we are

Juicy Tropic is a single-player garden harvest game. The core game runs on your device and does not require an account or an internet connection.

Data stored on your device

The app stores gameplay progress, settings, and an optional profile name and avatar image in local app storage. This data stays on your device unless you choose to share a screenshot or similar content yourself.

Camera and photos

If you set a profile photo, the app accesses the camera or photo library only after you choose that action. The selected image is saved locally as your avatar and is not uploaded by the game.

Network use

The app may use the network only to open Support or Privacy Policy pages when those links are configured. If the network is unavailable, this local page is shown instead.
